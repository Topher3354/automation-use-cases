# Automation use cases

An index of operational automation use-case writeups by [Christopher Ojo](https://github.com/Topher3354).

Each linked repository describes a problem, a workflow and the tools involved — typically n8n, Microsoft Power Automate, Microsoft 365 and Entra ID, Jira, or related systems. **These repositories are solution documentation, not application source code.**

They were first published on this GitHub account in March 2026. Three writeups are still a short outline only; those are marked below.

## IT operations and identity

- [IT service request auto-routing](https://github.com/Topher3354/IT-Service-Request-Auto-Routing) — read, categorise and route Jira service requests to the right assignee.
- [Automated employee onboarding pipeline](https://github.com/Topher3354/Automated-Employee-Onboarding-Pipeline) — provision accounts, licences and access when HR submits a new hire form.
- [Automated employee offboarding and access revocation](https://github.com/Topher3354/Automated-Employee-Offboarding-Access-Revocation) — disable accounts, revoke sessions and recover licences at exit, with an audit trail.
- [Device provisioning automation (Jira and Intune)](https://github.com/Topher3354/Device-Provisioning-Automation-Jira-Intune-) — connect approved Jira device requests to Microsoft Intune for profiles, compliance and apps.
- [IT incident triage and escalation](https://github.com/Topher3354/IT-Incident-Triage-Escalation-Bot) — classify Jira incident severity and escalate P1/P2 tickets to on-call engineers.
- [SLA monitoring and breach alerts](https://github.com/Topher3354/SLA-Monitoring-Breach-Alert-Automation) — watch open tickets against SLA deadlines, with warnings before a breach and escalation when one occurs.

## Finance, payments and staff requests

- [Vendor payment automation](https://github.com/Topher3354/Vendor-Payment-Automation-Pipeline) — from a structured payment request through validation, approval, payment and vendor confirmation.
- [Staff airtime and incentive disbursement portal](https://github.com/Topher3354/Staff-Airtime-Incentive-Disbursement-System-DAA-Portal-) — self-service staff requests, with identity checks and routed approval.
- [Invoice processing and approval](https://github.com/Topher3354/Invoice-Processing-Approval-Automation) — extract invoice data, validate vendors, route for approval and archive documents. *Short outline only; full writeup still to be added.*
- [Expense report validation and routing](https://github.com/Topher3354/Expense-Report-Auto-Validation-Routing) — check expense submissions against policy and route them to the right approver.
- [Finance reconciliation data pipeline](https://github.com/Topher3354/Finance-Reconciliation-Data-Pipeline) — match payment, internal and bank records and flag discrepancies for a reconciliation report.

## Sales, CRM and client work

- [CRM lead capture and enrichment](https://github.com/Topher3354/CRM-Lead-Capture-Enrichment-Automation) — capture leads, deduplicate against HubSpot, enrich, score and notify the assigned representative. *Short outline only; full writeup still to be added.*
- [Automated sales follow-up sequence](https://github.com/Topher3354/Automated-Sales-Follow-Up-Sequence) — behaviour-triggered follow-up email, pausing when the lead replies or books a call.
- [CRM data sync across platforms](https://github.com/Topher3354/CRM-Data-Sync-Across-Platforms) — keep customer data consistent across CRM, support, billing and internal systems.
- [Proposal and contract generation](https://github.com/Topher3354/Proposal-Contract-Generation-Automation) — populate a proposal or contract from CRM data, route for approval and send for signature. *Short outline only; full writeup still to be added.*
- [Customer onboarding workflow](https://github.com/Topher3354/Customer-Onboarding-Workflow-Automation) — at deal close, create tasks, notify teams, send a welcome and schedule the kickoff.

## Data and reporting

- [Automated operational reporting](https://github.com/Topher3354/Automated-Operational-Reporting-Pipeline) — collect operational data, calculate KPIs, refresh a report and email stakeholders.
- [Multi-source data aggregation](https://github.com/Topher3354/Multi-Source-Data-Aggregation-Transformation-Pipeline) — pull data from systems with different schemas and deliver a single structured dataset.

## About this index

This repository exists so the use cases can be browsed in one place. Nothing here is a live integration, and no client data is included.
